# Job sites for a remote video editor / motion designer

Checked live on 2026-09-28, logged out, from Bangladesh (Google localises there). Country-specific lines assume
Bangladesh; check eligibility for your own country.

## The rule that decides everything
**"Remote" usually means "remote inside one country".** On Working Nomads every editing job was locked to one
country (Edit Crew hires only in Latin America; most others are USA-only). Indeed, Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter remote
editing jobs are mostly US-residency. Always check who can apply before spending time on a post.

## Tier 3: skip (checked, not worth it)
- **Indeed / Glassdoor / ZipRecruiter / SimplyHired**: remote editor jobs are mostly US-residency. Indeed also bans automation.
- **Working Nomads**: every editing job is country-locked, and most are behind a paywall.
- **Wellfound**: tech startups; no video-editor listings.
- **Behance jobs**: the "video editing" filter returned design and illustration gigs.
- **Mandy / ProductionHUB**: film/TV crew work, mostly on-set, and Mandy needs a subscription.
- **PeoplePerHour**: a "video editor" search returned marketing and admin jobs.
- **Twine**: 0 video-editing jobs.
- **Remote OK / We Work Remotely / Jobicy / Dribbble**: ~1-2 editing jobs a month (already auto-scanned daily anyway).
- **youtubejobs.io**: re-posts YT Jobs listings. **Jooble**: aggregator of the boards above.
- **bdjobs**: 276 local listings, but local pay is far below the international boards.
- Reddit (r/HireAnEditor 70K, r/VideoEditor_forhire 78K, r/VideoEditingJobs 17K) is real, but Reddit is blocked on
  this network in both browsers. Check it on your phone.
